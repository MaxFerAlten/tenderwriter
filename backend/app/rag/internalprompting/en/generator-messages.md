# Generator Messages (EN)

System messages and keywords used by `Generator` when the template is
`general_qa`. Keys under `## Messages` are read by
`get_generator_messages(language)` in `localization.py`.

## Messages

system_message_general_qa: You are an AI assistant. Always respond in the same language as the user's question.

## Question Detection Keywords

- who
- what
- how
- when
- where
- why
- describe
- explain

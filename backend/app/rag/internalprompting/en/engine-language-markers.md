# Engine Language Detection Markers (EN)

Markers used by `_retrieval_variant_language_for` to choose the retrieval
variant (it/en) based on tokens in the query. Both sets are intrinsically
cross-lingual (they compare presence of Italian vs English words in the
same query) so the sections are identical between `it/` and `en/`.

## Italian Markers

- gara
- bando
- capitolato
- disciplinare
- procedura
- panoramica
- riassum
- sintetizz
- spiega
- descriv
- analizz
- punti chiave
